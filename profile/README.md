## What Is Frescobaldi

Frescobaldi is a Windows (and other) IDE for LilyPond. Engravers who already write `.ly` open it because a plain text editor does not show the page. Frescobaldi LilyPond editor is the buffer. Frescobaldi score preview is the PDF. Frescobaldi music engraving is the compile. Frescobaldi notation source is the text you actually own and version.

It is not Anvil Studio’s MIDI sequencer and not TablEdit’s guitar tab. LilyPond thinks in staves, paper size, and music typography. Choir librarians, contemporary composers, and edition projects you administer use this desk to keep source honest. The engine still has to be LilyPond; Frescobaldi runs it.

Work on scores you composed, arranged, or were hired to engrave. Keep includes with the project. Do not dump a copyrighted edition into source unless you have the right to typeset that work.

## Starting with Frescobaldi

Prerequisites: A PC you administer, LilyPond available to that seat, and a score you have the right to engrave.

1. **Prepare:** Point Frescobaldi LilyPond editor at the LilyPond binary you actually run.
2. **Configure:** Open the root `.ly` of a piece you own and set paper size for the players you will print.
3. **Test:** Compile once and confirm Frescobaldi score preview matches the notes you typed.
4. **Refine:** Click a collision on the page and fix Frescobaldi notation source, then compile again.
5. **Apply:** Grow Frescobaldi music engraving through the remaining movements of that work.
6. **Maintain:** Version the `.ly` tree with the PDFs, and keep copyrighted repertoire out unless licensed.
